Questions.

Does it come with cameras?

Yes. Choose eight HD bullet cameras or two to three 4K cameras, mounted on the box and ready to go. If you already have cameras you like, ask us about a box without them.

Do you install it?

The box arrives built, configured and tested, and you mount it. We install only when installation is quoted on your invoice.

What happens when the power goes out?

With the battery backup add-on, the battery takes over and keeps the box running for 30 minutes or more. The solar option does not depend on site power at all.

Where can I mount it?

Anywhere out of reach with power nearby: a temporary power pole, a post or a wall. When the job wraps, take it to the next one.

Can I order more than one?

Yes. Larger sites often run two or more, each with its own network name.
